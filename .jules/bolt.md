## 2025-05-18 - Pandas iloc vs NumPy Array Vectorization in Scanning Loops
**Learning:** Calling `df['High'].iloc[i]` or `df['Low'].iloc[i]` repeatedly inside loops per stock (such as counting near-test episodes across historical liquidity levels) introduces significant overhead from Pandas DataFrame/Series indexers (`frame.py:__getitem__` and `indexing.py:__getitem__`). Converting DataFrame series to NumPy arrays once (`df['High'].to_numpy(dtype=float)`) and performing vectorized slice operations yields a ~20x speedup for inner-loop functions, reducing overall per-stock scan time by ~35%.
**Action:** When evaluating historical OHLC bars inside loops across candidate levels, pre-extract NumPy arrays (`df['High'].to_numpy()`, `df['Low'].to_numpy()`) or pass pre-extracted arrays into helper functions to eliminate row-by-row Pandas indexer calls.
